Ride With Us

Group Rides from Ahmedabad

ThrotalYatry organizes premium motorcycle group rides for riders who crave open highways, scenic hill roads, and the camaraderie of fellow bikers. Every trip is led by experienced ride captains who know Gujarat's best routes — from the coastal highways to Dwarka and Somnath, the winding climbs to Saputara and Mount Abu, the surreal white desert of Kutch, the hidden trails of Polo Forest, and cross-border runs to Udaipur.

Whether you ride a Royal Enfield, adventure tourer, or sports bike, our trips are structured for safety, pacing, and unforgettable experiences. We handle route planning, fuel stops, meal halts, and backup support so you can focus on the ride.

Safety First

Safety Guidelines

Every ride follows strict safety protocols. Your wellbeing is our top priority on the road.

⛑️
ISI-certified helmet mandatory for all riders and pillions
🧤
Riding gloves, jacket, and closed shoes strongly recommended
🚦
Strict adherence to traffic rules and ride captain instructions
📏
Maintain staggered formation and safe following distance
🏥
First-aid kit and emergency contacts on every trip
🌧️
Rides rescheduled in severe weather — safety over schedule
🔋
Check fuel level, tyre pressure, and brakes before every departure
📱
Keep your phone charged and share your location with the group leader
Before You Ride

Rider Requirements

To join a ThrotalYatry group ride, please ensure you meet the following requirements. Our team will verify details when you call to register.

✓ Valid two-wheeler driving licence (LMV with gear)
✓ Own motorcycle in good working condition (or rent from ThrotalYatry)
✓ Valid vehicle registration, insurance & PUC certificate
✓ Minimum 1 year of on-road riding experience
✓ ISI-certified full-face or modular helmet
✓ Government-issued photo ID for registration
✓ Willingness to follow group ride rules and captain's lead
✓ Medical fitness — inform us of any health conditions in advance
