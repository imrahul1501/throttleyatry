Our Story is

Born on the Roads of Gujarat

ThrotalYatry started with a simple belief: every traveler deserves a safe, comfortable, and memorable journey through Gujarat — without the hassle of unreliable transport or confusing online bookings. Founded in Ahmedabad in 2014, we began with a handful of well-maintained cars and a passion for showing visitors the real Gujarat beyond the guidebooks.

Over the years, we grew from a small family operation into a full-service travel company offering bikes, cars, tempo travellers, and luxury buses. Today we serve thousands of tourists, pilgrims, corporate groups, and wedding parties every year — from Ahmedabad city tours to multi-day circuits covering Dwarka, Somnath, Gir, Kutch, and beyond.

What hasn't changed is our commitment to personal service. We don't believe in faceless transactions. Every booking starts with a conversation — understanding your needs, recommending the right vehicle, and building an itinerary that fits your pace and budget. That's the ThrotalYatry difference.
